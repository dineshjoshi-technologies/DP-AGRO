# MEMORANDUM OF UNDERSTANDING
## VIMEDIMEX-SUPERIOR-AGRITECH PARTNERSHIP

**Effective Date:** July 5, 2026
**Parties:**
- VIMEDIMEX (Vietnam Institute of Medicinal and Herbal Medicine and Pharmacy)
- Superior AgriTech Private Limited

---

## 1. PURPOSE AND OBJECTIVES

This Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) establishes a strategic partnership between VIMEDIMEX and Superior AgriTech for the development and commercialization of AI-powered medicinal agriculture in Vietnam, with focus on:

1. Establishing a joint R&D center for AI-driven phytochemical optimization
2. Expanding national-scale cultivation of medicinal plants
3. Developing export-focused supply chains targeting ASEAN and EU markets
4. Implementing advanced extraction and processing technologies

---

## 2. SCOPE OF PARTNERSHIP

### 2.1 Joint R&D Center
- **Location:** Hanoi, Vietnam
- **Focus Areas:** AI-driven phytochemical analysis, optimization, and extraction technology
- **Initial Projects:** Vietnamese ginseng, green tea, pandanus extracts, dragon's blood
- **IP Framework:** 50/50 joint ownership on co-developed innovations

### 2.2 Cultivation Network
- **Scale:** 5,000+ hectares under management
- **Crops:** Turmeric, ginseng, green tea, pandan leaves, dragon's blood
- **Technology:** Sensor networks, AI yield prediction, quality monitoring
- **Certification:** Organic, GMP, ISO 9001:2015 aligned

### 2.3 Export & Distribution
- **Target Markets:** ASEAN (priority), EU (pharmaceutical grade), US (nutraceutical)
- **Logistics:** State-backed distribution network
- **Branding:** Co-branded premium extracts

### 2.4 Technology Transfer
- **Extraction:** Supercritical CO2, molecular distillation
- **Processing:** Automated quality control systems
- **Data:** Shared analytics platform for quality traceability

---

## 3. GOVERNANCE STRUCTURE

### 3.1 Steering Committee
- **Composition:** 6 members (3 from each party)
- **Chairperson:** CEO - Superior AgriTech
- **Members:**
  - Superior AgriTech: CEO, CTO, Head of R&D
  - VIMEDIMEX: Institute Director, Director of Agri-Tech, Head of Export

### 3.2 Decision-Making
- Consensus required for strategic decisions
- Majority vote for operational decisions
- Escalation to CEO for urgent matters

### 3.3 Meeting Schedule
- Biweekly operational meetings (30 mins)
- Quarterly strategic roundtables (2 hrs)
- Ad-hoc meetings as needed

---

## 4. FINANCIAL TERMS

### 4.1 Initial Investment
- **$2.5M** joint R&D fund (Year 1)

### 4.2 Revenue Sharing
- **60/40** (VIMEDIMEX/Superior AgriTech) on export sales

### 4.3 Pricing
- Premium pricing for AI-verified quality batches (+25% margin)
