# VIMEDIMEX-Superior-AgriTech Joint Steering Committee Charter

## Governance Structure
- **Chairperson**: [CEO - Supervisor-AgriTech]
- **Members**:
  - 3 from Supervisor-AgriTech (CEO, CTO, Head of R&D)
  - 3 from VIMEDIMEX (Institute Director, Director of Agri-Tech, Head of Export)

## Responsibilities
1. Strategic Direction
   - Oversee partnership alignment with company mission
   - Approve major investment commitments
   - Monitor progress against 3-year plan milestones

2. Technical Oversight
   - Review R&D project proposals
   - Approve technology integration protocols
   - Monitor data sharing frameworks

3. Quality Assurance
   - Review certification processes
   - Approve quality control standards
   - Monitor export compliance

4. Financial Management
   - Track budget allocations
   - Review financial reports
   - Approve revenue-sharing arrangements

## Decision-Making Process
- Consensus required for strategic decisions
- Majority vote for operational decisions
- Escalation path to CEO for urgent matters

## Meeting Calendar
- Biweekly operational meetings (30 mins)
- Quarterly strategic roundtables (2 hrs)
- Ad-hoc meetings as needed

## IP & Data Framework
- Joint IP ownership for co-developed innovations
- Shared data lake access with usage rights defined
- Confidentiality agreements for sensitive information

## Dispute Resolution
- Mediation process via Supervisor-AgriTech legal team
- Escalation to governing body if unresolved within 15 days
