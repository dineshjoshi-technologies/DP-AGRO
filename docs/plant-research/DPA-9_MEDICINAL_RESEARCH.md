# DPA-9 Medicinal Plant Research Plan

## Objectives
- Conduct therapeutic and market research for key medicinal herbs (Neem, Tulsi, Ashwagandha, Turmeric, etc.).
- Establish evidence‑based product concepts and target markets.
- Compile regulatory, supply‑chain, and competitive insights.

## Research Phases
1. **Literature Review**
   - Academic journals, patent databases, and traditional medicine texts.
   - Summarize pharmacological mechanisms, clinical evidence, and dosage forms.

2. **Market Landscape**
   - Current market size, growth trends, and segmentation.
   - Competitive analysis of existing products in each herb category.

3. **Regulatory & Quality**
   - Local and international standards (FDA, ISO, GMP, etc.).
   - Key quality attributes and required testing protocols.

4. **Supply Chain Assessment**
   - Principal cultivators, harvest seasons, and post‑harvest handling.
   - Identification of single‑source risks and mitigation strategies.

5. **Product Ideation & Validation**
   - Formulate product prototypes (capsules, teas, topical). 
   - Design early‑stage pilot trials or consumer feedback loops.
