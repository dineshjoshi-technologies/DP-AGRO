# Partner Evaluation Framework with Tiered Ranking

## Purpose
Standardized methodology for evaluating, ranking, and categorizing strategic partners across Southeast Asia for the AI-powered Medicinal Agriculture Platform.

## Scope
Applies to all potential and active partners in Thailand, Vietnam, Philippines, Malaysia, Indonesia.

---

## Tier Definitions

### Tier 1: Strategic Anchor Partners
**Criteria:** Market leadership, regulatory alignment, infrastructure scale, long-term strategic value
- **Investment:** High (co-investment, joint R&D, revenue sharing)
- **Governance:** Joint steering committee, board-level engagement
- **Duration:** 5+ years with renewal clauses
- **Max Partners:** 3-5 total across region

## Evaluation Scoring Methodology

### Dimension Weights (Total = 100%)

| Dimension | Weight | Sub-Criteria |
|-----------|--------|--------------|
| **Strategic Alignment** | 25% | Mission fit, long-term vision compatibility, exclusivity potential |
| **Market Access & Reach** | 20% | Distribution channels, farmer network, regulatory licenses |
| **Technical & R&D Capability** | 20% | Research infrastructure, IP portfolio, AI/data readiness |
| **Operational Infrastructure** | 15% | Manufacturing capacity, quality systems, digital maturity |
| **ESG & Sustainability** | 10% | Carbon footprint, regenerative practices, social impact |
| **Financial Health & Stability** | 10% | Revenue trends, funding, audit history, risk profile |

### Scoring Scale (1-100)

| Score Range | Classification | Action |
|-------------|----------------|--------|
| 90-100 | Tier 1 - Strategic Anchor | Immediate MoU negotiation |
| 80-89 | Tier 2 - Capability Partner | Pilot design & technical validation |
| 70-79 | Tier 3 - Ecosystem Partner | Capacity building & network integration |
| 60-69 | Watch List | Monitor, reassess in 12 months |
| <60 | Do Not Pursue | Document rationale, archive |

## Evaluation Process

### Phase 1: Screening (Week 1-2)
1. Complete Partner Intake Form (Appendix A)
2. Automated scoring against Tier 3 minimum threshold (70)
3. Disqualify <60, flag 60-69 for monitoring

### Phase 2: Deep Dive (Week 3-6)
1. Site visits / virtual audits
2. Technical capability assessment
3. Legal & compliance review
4. Financial due diligence
5. ESG assessment (carbon, labor, biodiversity)

### Phase 3: Committee Review (Week 7)
1. Evaluation Committee scores independently
2. Calibration meeting to resolve variances >10 points
3. Final tier assignment documented

### Phase 4: Approval (Week 8)
1. CMO presents framework results to Executive Committee
2. CEO sign-off on Tier 1 partners
3. CFO budget confirmation for engagement tier

## Tier-Specific Engagement Models

### Tier 1: Strategic Anchor
- **Structure:** Joint Venture / Strategic Alliance Agreement
- **IP Framework:** Co-development with shared ownership
- **Data Sharing:** Federated learning nodes, shared data lake
- **Governance:** Quarterly Executive Steering Committee
- **KPIs:** Revenue growth, joint IP filings, farmer reach, carbon reduction

### Tier 2: Capability Partner
- **Structure:** Master Services Agreement + Project SOWs
- **IP Framework:** Project-specific IP assignment
- **Data Sharing:** API-based, purpose-limited
- **Governance:** Monthly project reviews, quarterly business reviews
- **KPIs:** Pilot milestones, technical metrics, time-to-value

### Tier 3: Ecosystem Partner
- **Structure:** MoU + Capacity Building Agreement
- **IP Framework:** Background IP retained, foreground IP to company
- **Data Sharing:** Aggregated insights only, no raw data
- **Governance:** Semi-annual reviews, annual planning
- **KPIs:** Farmer onboarding, adoption rates, sustainability metrics

## Re-Ranking & Review Cadence

| Trigger | Review Scope | Timeline |
|---------|--------------|----------|
| **Annual Strategic Review** | All tiers | Q1 each year |
| **Pilot Completion** | Tier 2 → Tier 1 promotion | Within 30 days |
| **Material Change** (M&A, regulation, leadership) | Affected partner | Within 14 days |
| **KPI Miss (2 consecutive quarters)** | Tier 1/2 partners | Within 30 days |
| **New Market Entry** | New country partners | Before engagement |
