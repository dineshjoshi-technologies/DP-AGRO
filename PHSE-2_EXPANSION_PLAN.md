## Risk Management Framework

### Technical Risks (High Probability)

**Risk**: AI Model Scalability
- **Impact**: Production delays at scale
- **Mitigation**: Federated deployment, stress testing
- **Contingency**: Multi-model backup strategy

**Risk**: Manufacturing Process Optimization
- **Impact**: Quality issues during expansion
- **Mitigation**: Digital twin simulation before deployment
- **Contingency**: Phased rollout with pilot facilities

### Market Risks (Medium Probability)

**Risk**: International Trade Barriers
- **Impact**: Export delays and cost increases
- **Mitigation**: Local partnerships, dual sourcing
- **Contingency**: Region diversification strategy

**Risk**: Farmer Adoption Resistance
- **Impact**: Network effects not achieved
- **Mitigation**: Training programs, incentive structures
- **Contingency**: Hybrid model with traditional channels

### ESG Risks (High Impact)

**Risk**: Environmental Compliance Failure
- **Impact**: Reputation damage, legal penalties
- **Mitigation**: Continuous monitoring, third-party audits
- **Contingency**: Immediate remediation protocols

**Risk**: Social License Withdrawal
- **Impact**: Farmer resistance, NGO opposition
- **Mitigation**: Transparent impact reporting, community engagement
- **Contingency**: Alternative partnership models
