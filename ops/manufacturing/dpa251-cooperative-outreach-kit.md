# Karnataka Farmer Cooperative Outreach Kit — DPA-251 / DPA-235 SC-01

**Status:** KIT READY — no outreach sent yet, no cooperative named as partner
**Date:** 2026-09-15
**Prepared by:** Operations Sustainability Lead (DPA-76)
**Consumes:** DPA-235 written qualification criteria (ops/manufacturing/dpa235-supply-chain-partner-shortlist.md, SC-01)

## Purpose

Operationalizes the DPA-76 supply-chain engagement for farmer cooperatives /
FPOs in the four Karnataka agro-climatic zones (A–D) covering FARM-001..010.
Produces the named cooperative list with registration references required for
[DPA-149-STATE-001](dpa149-permit-state-application.md) attachment, without
fabricating cooperative names or registration numbers.

## Target zones and membership footprint

| Zone | Farms covered | Proposed coverage target |
|---|---|---|
| A | FARM-001, FARM-005, FARM-009 | ≥2 cooperatives/FPOs |
| B | FARM-002, FARM-006, FARM-010 | ≥2 cooperatives/FPOs |
| C | FARM-003, FARM-007 | ≥1 cooperative/FPO |
| D | FARM-004, FARM-008 | ≥1 cooperative/FPO |
| Cross-zone | All | ≥1 species overlay cooperative per botanical species |

Critical-input rule: **≥2 accredited sources per species OR CEO-accepted single-source risk.**

## Step 1 — Registry screen (no fabrication)

Candidates must be confirmed against public records before naming them as DPA
partners. Sources:

1. Karnataka State Co-operative Societies portal (RCS Karnataka) — verify
   registration number + active status.
2. SFAC / NABARD FPO lists for Karnataka (Farmer Producer Organisations).
3. District Cooperative Agriculture / Horticulture Societies in
   Bangalore Rural, Ramanagara, Tumakuru, Kolar, Chikkaballapur districts
   (proximity to farm coordinates).
4. Organic producer / medicinal-plant grower clusters in the Bangalore plateau.

Each candidate gets a `KA-COOP-<zone>-<n>` ref. Only verified registrations
carry a "verified" status; everything else is "candidate — verification pending".

## Step 2 — Qualification questionnaire (mapped to DPA-235 criteria)

Send to qualifying candidates; collect evidence:

1. RCS Karnataka registration certificate (name, number, date, active status).
2. Charter/membership: member farmer count, land under cultivation, species grown.
3. Cultivation practice: organic / GAP / conventional; certifications held.
4. Plot-level traceability capability (link to FARM-001..010 zone).
5. Named supply capacity per species per harvest window (Oct 2026 – May 2027).
6. Quality agreement willingness (botanical ID, moisture, contamination specs).
7. Governance: board contact, conflict-of-interest declaration.

## Step 3 — Qualification decision (written)

Per DPA-235 criteria: registration + cultivation cert/audit + traceability +
capacity + quality agreement + resilience (dual-source). A "qualified"
designation requires all evidence on file; otherwise stays "candidate".
