**Prepared by:** Operations Sustainability Lead (DPA-76 / DPA-235)

## Purpose

Identify and qualify supply chain partners for the Phase 1 pilot manufacturing
facility's critical inputs: **raw botanical material, food-grade packaging,
process solvents, and process equipment**. Every listed partner is either a
candidate (not yet qualified) or qualified (scored against written criteria
with evidence). No candidate is upgraded to "qualified" without a scoring
record. Dual-source coverage is evaluated per critical input.

## Written Qualification Criteria

Cross-cutting requirements apply to all partner categories:

- Registered legal entity with valid incorporation/registration documentation
- No sanctions, adverse media, or regulatory enforcement history (screened at onboarding)
- Financial viability (for suppliers of goods/services over $25K/yr estimated spend)
- Environmental compliance: pollution control consent (India: CTE/CTO), waste handling permits
- Written commitment to DPA quality/ESG standards; training record for facility staff
- Conflict-of-interest declaration; no ties to DPA competitors in same product line

### 1. Raw Botanical Material (critical, single-source risk HIGH pre-coverage)

Applies to farmer cooperatives / FPOs and contract cultivators supplying
neem, tulsi, stevia, moringa, amla, aloe (Phase 1 botanical set, per
plant-research/raw-data).

| Criterion | Requirement | Verify via |
|---|---|---|
| Legal form | Registered cooperative (Karnataka Co-operative Societies Act 1959) or registered FPO | RCS Karnataka registration certificate |
| Cultivation | Organic or GAP-certified (or credible third-party audit); no banned agrochemical residues | Certification / audit report; residue lab result at first batch |
| Traceability | Plot-level lot traceability back to farm ID | Farm registry linkage (FARM-001..010), lot records |
| Supply capacity | Nameplate volume ≥ DPA monthly requirement per species; harvest calendar aligned to Oct 2026–May 2027 window | Cooperative charter + member-plot data |
| Quality agreement | Written stewardship + quality agreement incl. botanical ID, moisture, contamination specs | Executed agreement |
| Resilience | ≥ 2 accredited sources per species OR written risk acceptance with CEO sign-off | Dual-source matrix (below) |

### 2. Food-Grade Packaging (critical)

PCH/PET jars, closures, laminated foil, child-resistant options for
tinctures/oils/powders, plus shipping cartons. India FSSAI + BIS contact
material compliance.

| Criterion | Requirement | Verify via |
|---|---|---|
| Food-contact compliance | BIS/IS 9873 or equivalent food-contact certification; FSSAI registered manufacturer | Certificate + FSSAI license |
| Quality systems | ISO 9001 (or equivalent) manufacturing; incoming QC data per lot | Certificate + QC records |
| Hygiene | Clean-room / dust-controlled production environment | Audit report |
| Resilience | ≥ 2 approved packaging suppliers, or dual-region sourcing | Dual-source matrix |
| Logistics | Reliable lead time ≤ 4 weeks for standard SKUs | Order history / commitment letter |

### 3. Process Solvents (critical)

Food-grade ethanol (extraction/solvent), CO2 for supercritical extraction.

| Criterion | Requirement | Verify via |
|---|---|---|
| Grade | Food-grade / USP-NF (ethanol), food-grade CO2 (IS 3076 purity classes) | COA per lot |
| Regulatory | Excise-licensed ethanol supplier (India: state excise), food-grade CO2 producer | License |
| Supply security | ≥ 2 qualified suppliers per solvent | Dual-source matrix |
| Safety | MSDS, hazard-compliant transport, storage support | MSDS + transport docs |

### 4. Process Equipment (capital, vendor-agnostic)

CO2 extractors, dryers, centrifuges, milling/pulverizers, blending tanks, HPLC/QC,
ISO 7 HVAC, packaging line (per MANUFACTURING_SCALING_PLAN).

| Criterion | Requirement | Verify via |
|---|---|---|
| Vendor-agnostic spec | Bid against DPA equipment spec sheet, not brand preference; energy-efficiency targets drive selection | Tender spec + bid comparison |
| Energy spec | Energy efficiency target per equipment class (per energy-audit planning baseline) | Spec sheet + sub-metering plan |
| QA/compliance | GMP-appropriate construction materials; validation package (IQ/OQ/PQ) | Validation docs |
| Support | Spare parts + service in India ≤ 48h response | Service agreement |
| Track record | ≥ 3 reference installations for same equipment class | References |
