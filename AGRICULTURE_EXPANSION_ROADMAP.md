# AGRICULTURE_EXPANSION_ROADMAP

## Executive Summary
A strategic plan to scale medicinal agriculture operations across phases, aligning with company mission to advance health through sustainable agricultural innovations.

## Roadmap Overview
- **Mission Alignment**: Accelerate R&D-to-market translation for plant-derived therapeutics
- **Timeline**: 4-year multi-phase approach
- **Budget**: $25M total allocation
- **Success Metrics**: 300% revenue growth, 50% reduction in R&D timelines

## Phase 1: Planning (0-12 months)
### Deliverables
- Market assessment report
- Regulatory compliance framework
- Stakeholder roadmap
### KPIs
- 90% stakeholder approval
- $5M budget secured
### Resources
- Project manager, 3 researchers, legal advisor

## Phase 2: Pilot (12-24 months)
### Deliverables
- Functional pilot farm
- Initial product batches
- Data analytics dashboard
### KPIs
- 80% yield consistency
- 2 clinical trials completed
### Resources
- 5 agronomists, 2 production managers

## Phase 3: Scale (24-36 months)
### Deliverables
- 3 commercial farms
- Supply chain integration
-Distribution partnerships
### KPIs
- $15M annual revenue
- 95% customer satisfaction
### Resources
- 8 production staff, logistics partners

## Phase 4: Global Expansion (36-48 months)
### Deliverables
- Entry into 5 international markets
- Regulatory partnerships

Global distribution network
### KPIs
- 10 countries operational
- Break-even achieved
### Resources
- International consultants, 10 sales reps

## Risk Mitigation
- Regulatory diversifiers across geographies
- Diversified crop portfolio
- Contingency budget ($2M)

## Success Metrics
- 300% revenue growth
- 50% faster R&D cycles
- 20+ patents filed
