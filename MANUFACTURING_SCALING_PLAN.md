# Manufacturing Scaling Plan

## Executive Summary
Scale manufacturing capacity to meet projected demand growth while maintaining quality standards and sustainability commitments. Target: 300% capacity increase over 36 months.

## Current State Assessment
- Current capacity: 500,000 kg annual production
- Utilization rate: 75%
- Available capacity: 166,667 kg annually
- Primary bottlenecks: drying, extraction, packaging
- Quality compliance: 99.8% pass rate

## Scaling Phases

### Phase 1: Near-term (0-12 months)
**Capacity Target:** 750,000 kg annually (+50%)

**Key Initiatives:**
- Install 2 additional automated drying systems ($450K)
- Expand extraction capacity with 2 new CO2 extractors ($1.2M)
- Implement automated packaging line ($800K)
- Upgrade quality control lab ($350K)
- Hire 25 additional manufacturing staff

**Investment:** $2.8M
**Expected ROI:** 18 months

### Phase 2: Mid-term (12-24 months)
**Capacity Target:** 1,250,000 kg annually (+150%)

**Key Initiatives:**
- Build Phase 2 facility (50,000 sq ft) ($8M)
- Install continuous extraction systems ($2.5M)
- Implement AI-powered quality control ($600K)
- Deploy warehouse automation ($400K)
- Establish raw material storage expansion ($300K)

**Investment:** $11.8M
**Expected ROI:** 24 months

### Phase 3: Long-term (24-36 months)
**Capacity Target:** 2,000,000 kg annually (+300%)

**Key Initiatives:**
- Launch Phase 3 facility (80,000 sq ft) ($12M)
- Implement full production line automation ($3M)
- Deploy predictive maintenance AI ($500K)
- Integrate supply chain optimization ($1M)
- Establish export packaging capabilities ($800K)

**Investments:** $17.3M
**Expected ROI:** 30 months
