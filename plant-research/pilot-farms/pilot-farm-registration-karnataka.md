## Scope

All 10 Phase 1 pilot farms of the medicinal plant genomics sampling program
(Oct 2026 – May 2027 collection window) are located in **Karnataka**
(Bangalore plateau, ~12.3–13.4°N / 76.9–78.5°E). No pilot farm exists in
Maharashtra, Tamil Nadu, Gujarat, Uttar Pradesh, or Rajasthan; those states
remain provisional expansion candidates and are excluded from this filing until
pilot farms are established there.
