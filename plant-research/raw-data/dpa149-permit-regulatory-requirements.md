## 2. Key Regulatory Frameworks

### 2.1 Biological Diversity Act, 2002 (BDA)
- **NBA approval required** before accessing biological resources or associated traditional knowledge
- **Prior Informed Consent (PIC)** from local communities if traditional knowledge involved
- **Benefit Sharing Agreement** — 1-10% of commercial benefits to local communities/trust
- **State Biodiversity Boards** administer state-level compliance

### 2.2 Environment Protection Act, 1986
- **GEAC** (Genetic Engineering Appraisal Committee) under MOEFCC
- **RCGM** (Review Committee on Genetic Manipulation) under DBT for research biosafety
- **IBSC** (Institutional Biosafety Committee) at institutional level
- BSL-1, BSL-2 containment requirements depending on organism modification level

### 2.3 Drugs and Cosmetics Act, 1940 / DCGI
- If any pharmaceutical-grade research or clinical correlation
- **New Drug/Clinical Trial** registration if testing plant-derived compounds

### 2.4 ICAR / Indian Council of Agricultural Research
- Research project registration
- Field trial permits
- Seed/genotype access agreements

### 2.5 NMPB (National Medicinal Plants Board)
- Medicinal plant research framework
- Good Agricultural and Collection Practices (GACP) compliance
- Quality control standards for raw materials

## 3. Required Documentation

### 3.1 Permit Application Checklist

- [ ] Research proposal with objectives, methodology, timeline
- [ ] Plant species list with scientific names and quantities needed
- [ ] Biosafety risk assessment (for genomics manipulation work)
- [ ] Material Transfer Agreement (MTA) draft
- [ ] Institutional Biosafety Committee (IBC) registration certificate
- [ ] Prior Informed Consent (PIC) documentation (if traditional knowledge involved)
- [ ] Benefit Sharing Agreement (BSA) draft (under BDA 2002)
- [ ] State Forest Department clearance (if wild collection involved)
- [ ] CITES export/import permit (if international collaboration)
- [ ] Ethics committee clearance (if human samples or traditional knowledge)
- [ ] Data sharing and IP agreement (if joint research with external institution)
