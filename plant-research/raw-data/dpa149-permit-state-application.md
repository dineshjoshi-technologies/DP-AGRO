## 3. Research Activities Permitted
The following activities are requested under this permit:
- **Non-destructive sampling:** Leaf and seed collection from cultivated plants (max 20% per plant per season)
- **Genomic DNA extraction:** Laboratory processing of collected samples
- **Field observations:** Phenotyping, growth measurements, harvest timing
- **Soil and water sampling:** Physico-chemical analysis from farm plots
- **Pest and disease monitoring:** Visual assessment and sample collection
- **Harvest data collection:** Yield per plant, biomass measurements
- **NO destructive sampling:** Roots not excavated; NO plant uprooting permitted
- **NO field experiments:** No planting of new genotypes; no fertilizer/ pesticide trials

## 4. Plant Material and Quantities
- **Per Farm:** 50 plants per species × 6 species = 300 plants maximum per DPA pilot farm
- **Per State:** Estimated 5-10 DPA-partnered farms per state
- **Total across states:** ~1,500-3,000 plants (cultivated, marked for observation only)
- **Collection Window:** Post-monsoon (Oct-Nov 2026) through summer (Mar-May 2027)
- **Sampling Frequency:** Once per growth phase (vegetative, flowering, fruiting)
