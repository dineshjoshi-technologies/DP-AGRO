## 2. Research Objectives
1. Generate high-quality genomic sequences for six target medicinal plant species
2. Map active compound biosynthesis gene clusters
3. Develop molecular markers for cultivar authentication and quality standardization
4. Enable AI model training for predictive breeding and compound enhancement
5. Support sustainable cultivation recommendations based on genomic insights

## 4. Research Methodology
- **Genomic DNA Extraction:** CTAB method + column-based purification
- **Library Preparation:** Illumina TruSeq Nano DNA Library Prep Kit
- **Sequencing Platform:** Illumina NovaSeq 6000 (2×150 bp, ~30x coverage)
- **Bioinformatics Pipeline:** Adapter trimming → quality control → de novo assembly → annotation
- **No Genetic Modification:** Sequencing-only work; no CRISPR, no transgenic construction
- **Data Deposition:** NCBI GenBank accessions deposited upon completion

## 5. Biosafety and Containment

- **Environmental Release:** Not applicable (laboratory-based sequencing only)
- **Waste Management:** Standard biochemical waste disposal; autoclaving of all biological waste
