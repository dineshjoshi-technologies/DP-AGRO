## 1. Project Summary
- **Project:** Medicinal Plant Genomics Research — AI-Enabled Cultivar Improvement
- **Phase:** DPA-75 Phase 1
- **Objective:** Generate genomic resources for six priority medicinal plant species to support AI-driven compound enhancement and cultivar authentication
- **Duration:** 12 months (permit period: 6 months initial + 6 months extension)

## 2. Regulatory Basis
- **Guideline Reference:** DBT Guidelines for Research in Genomic/DNA (2019)
- **Applicability:** All genomics research involving plant DNA/RNA sequencing
- **Clearance Required:** Biosafety clearance for non-GMO genomic sequencing work
- **Submission Portal:** DBT-BIRAC Online Research Proposal System

## 3. Research Details
- **Genome Sequencing:** Whole-genome shotgun sequencing of 6 medicinal plant species
- **Sequencing Depth:** 30x average coverage per species
- **Technology:** Illumina short-read sequencing; assembly and annotation bioinformatics pipeline
- **Data Type:** Raw reads, assembled genomes, gene annotations, compound biosynthesis pathway maps
- **Intermediates:** Genomic DNA extractions (plant tissue → DNA → purified DNA)
- **Final Data:** Sequence files, genome assemblies, annotation files (GFF/GBK format)

## 4. Biosafety Assessment
- **Risk Level:** BSL-1 (plant genomic DNA; no human, animal, or pathogen sequences)
- **Modification Status:** Sequencing-only; NO genetic modification, no transgenic construction
- **IBSC Clearance:** DPA Institutional Biosafety Committee clearance PENDING (Protocol: DPA-IBSC-2026-01)
- **RCGM Status:** Not required — no recombinant DNA or GM organism generation
- **Containment:** Standard laboratory practices; no environmental release of any modified material
- **Waste Disposal:** Autoclave at 121°C for 30 min; chemical decontamination of DNA waste

## 5. Data Management and Sharing
- **Primary Data Storage:** DPA secure servers (encrypted, access-controlled)
- **Public Deposition:** NCBI GenBank upon publication (6 genome accessions expected)
- **Interim Sharing:** No interim data sharing planned; all analysis performed on DPA internal compute
- **Metadata Standards:** MIAME/MIA minimal information standards for genomic data
- **Sequence Read Archive:** SRA submission planned within 6 months of sequencing completion

- **Facilities:** DPA certified molecular genomics laboratory
- **Equipment:** Sequencing instrument access, compute cluster (20+ cores, 128GB RAM)
