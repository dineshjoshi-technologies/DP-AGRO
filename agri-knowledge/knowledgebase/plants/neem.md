# Neem Therapeutic Research

## Traditional Uses
- Antiseptic properties
- Wound healing
- Diarrhea treatment
- Skin infections and fungal disorders
- Malaria treatment (leaf extracts)
- Dental health applications (mouthwash, toothpaste)

## Modern Applications
- Anti-inflammatory (contains nimbin)
- Diabetes management (nimbinide)
- Antibacterial (neemburol)
- Antimalarial properties
- Cancer prevention research (nitrogen-containing compounds)
- Hormone regulation
- Cardiovascular benefits
- Immune system modulation

## Research Gaps
- Standardized dosage for diabetes supplements
- Long-term safety studies
- Clinical trials for cancer applications
- Comparative effectiveness vs. conventional treatments
- Optimal formulation for delivery systems
- Pediatric dosing guidelines
- Drug interaction profiles with conventional medications
- Large-scale human trials for chronic conditions
