# Moringa Research Gaps

## Data Limitations
- Limited peer-reviewed studies on Hai Yog -based formulations
- Inconsistent standardization of moringa oleifera leaf extraction methods
- Missing clinical trial data for blood sugar regulation claims

##regulatory Uncertainties
- VaryingFDA guidelines for herbal supplement labeling
- Lack of standardized moisture content requirements

##Scalability Challenges
- Limited industrial-scale processing capacity for moringa leaf powder
- High moisture content affecting shelf life
