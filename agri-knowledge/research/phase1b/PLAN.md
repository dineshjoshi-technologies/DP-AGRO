# Phase 1b: Therapeutic & Market Research

## Plants: Neem, Tulsi, Moringa, Stevia, Amla, AloeVera

## Objective
Establish comprehensive therapeutic and market research foundation for six high-value medicinal plants to support AI-driven cultivation optimization, product development, and export strategy.

## Research Framework

### 1. Neem (Azadirachta indica)
**Therapeutic Properties:**
- Antimicrobial, antifungal, antibacterial
- Antioxidant, anti-inflammatory
- Immunomodulatory effects
- Pest deterrent properties

**Market Analysis:**
- Global market: $8.2B (2024)
- Key markets: US, EU, Japan, GCC
- Primary applications: Natural pesticides, cosmetics, pharmaceuticals

### 2. Tulsi (Ocimum tenuiflorum)
**Therapeutic Properties:**
- Adaptogenic, anxiolytic
- Immunostimulant, antihypertensive
- Antimicrobial, antioxidant
- Respiratory support

**Market Analysis:**
- Global market: $5.8B (2024)
- Key markets: US, EU, India domestic
- Primary applications: Teas, supplements, aromatherapy

### 3. Moringa (Moringa oleifera)
**Therapeutic Properties:**
- Nutrient-dense (vitamins A, C, E, B-complex)
- Antioxidant, anti-inflammatory
- Liver protectant, cardioprotective
- Protein-rich seeds

**Market Analysis:**
- Global market: $7.9B (2024)
- Key markets: US, Africa, Middle East
- Primary applications: Food supplements, animal feed, cosmetics

### 4. Stevia (Stevia rebaudiana)
**Therapeutic Properties:**
- Non-nutritive sweetener (200-300x sweeter than sucrose)
- Antioxidant, anti-inflammatory
- Glycemic control support
- Dental health benefits

**Market Analysis:**
- Global market: $3.2B (2024)
- Key markets: US, EU, China
- Primary applications: Food & beverage, pharmaceuticals

### 5. Amla (Phyllanthus emblica)
**Therapeutic Properties:**
- Rich in vitamin C (up to 60mg/g)
- Antioxidant, hepatoprotective
- Immunomodulatory, anti-diabetic
- Gastrointestinal health

**Market Analysis:**
- Global market: $2.1B (2024)
- Key markets: India domestic, US, EU
- Primary applications: Supplements, juices, traditional medicines

### 6. Aloe Vera (Aloe barbadensis)
**Therapeutic Properties:**
- Wound healing, skin regeneration
- Anti-inflammatory, antimicrobial
- Hydration, soothing effects
- Digestive health (leaf gel)

**Market Analysis:**
- Global market: $9.4B (2024)
- Key markets: US, EU, Asia-Pacific
- Primary applications: Cosmetics, pharmaceuticals, food

## Research Deliverables

1. **Therapeutic Profiles** - Documented active compounds, mechanisms, clinical evidence
2. **Cultivation Requirements** - Climate, soil, water, agrochemical needs
3. **Processing Methods** - Extraction, preservation, standardization protocols
4. **Market Intelligence** - Demand trends, regulatory status, export potential
5. **AI Integration Points** - Data requirements for machine learning models
6. **Quality Standards** - ISO, pharmacopoeia references, certification pathways

## Knowledge Object Structure

Each plant will have:
- `/therapeutic.md` - Active compounds, traditional uses, clinical studies
- `/cultivation.md` - Growing requirements, yield optimization, pest management
- `/processing.md` - Harvesting, extraction, standardization methods
- `/market.md` - Demand analysis, price trends, export regulations
- `/quality.md` - Standards, testing protocols, certification requirements

## Success Metrics

- Complete therapeutic and market profiles for all 6 plants
- Establish AI-ready data structures for each plant
- Identify 3-5 high-value product opportunities per plant
- Map regulatory pathways for export markets
- Create foundation for Phase 2: AI Training Dataset Creation
